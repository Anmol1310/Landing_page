Solving the Hidden Pain Points in Modern POS Systems

Built by Anmol Hiteshkumar Brahmbhatt

🚀 Project Overview

This end-to-end POS platform reimagines restaurant operations with: real-time ordering, smart kitchen dashboards, AI inventory management, and role-based apps for admin, kitchen, and customer users.

⚙️ Key Features

  • Customer App: Menu browsing, video previews, order customization, feedback
  • Kitchen/Admin App: Order queue, inventory tracking, table & employee management
  • AI Inventory: Voice input like “Add 1 box of tomatoes” updates stock instantly
  • Realtime Sync: Firebase Firestore-based order and table state updates

➗ Problem in Paper based menu

  • No Real-Time Updates – Prices, availability, and specials can’t be updated instantly, leading to errors.
  • High Printing Costs – Frequent reprints for changes or damaged menus add up.
  • Limited Engagement – No videos, images, or interactive content to attract customers.
  • Hygiene Concerns – Shared menus can spread germs, especially in a post-COVID world.
  • Waste & Environmental Impact – Continuous paper use contributes to environmental waste.
  • Slow Ordering Process – Customers can’t order directly; they must wait for staff to take orders.
  • No Data Collection – Lacks integration with inventory, sales analytics, and feedback systems.

✍ Advantages of POS

  • Faster Service & Efficiency – Streamlined ordering, kitchen communication, and billing reduce wait times.
  • Interactive Digital Menus – Customers can view images, videos, and details before ordering, enhancing their experience.
  • Real-Time Inventory & Sales Insights – Helps owners make data-driven decisions and cut down waste.
  • Contactless & Convenient – Safer, faster payments and reduced dependency on paper menus.
